# 현대 지리 레이어 연동 (부분 구현)
`public/data/henan-central-river.json`, `henan-central-road.json` are optional GeoJSON FeatureCollections, clipped to region bounds, WGS84 longitude/latitude, LineString features. Top-level provenance requires kind=modern, source, license. No real dataset is bundled or verified. Candidate OpenStreetMap (ODbL; attribution and derivative database obligations require review before distribution). Forest polygons and clipping/conversion are not implemented. Missing layers stay empty; never substitute fictional rivers. Line heights follow sampled DEM at vertices only; not water depth or historical alignment. Cost overlay is provisional elevation/slope classification, not measured land cover; no deep-water inference. Thresholds 0.75 cliff, 0.2 mountain, 650m mountain, 0.05 and 200m plain are developer assumptions. Overlay grid 65² is coarse and can intersect terrain. No routing.
